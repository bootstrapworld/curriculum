= Standards dictionaries

Each `standards-*-dictionary.json` file maps a standard's ID to its text and to the lessons aligned with it:

[source,json]
----
"6.12A": {
  "description": "Represent numeric data graphically, including dot plots, ...",
  "lessons": ["dot-plots", "histograms-visualize"]
}
----

The files are registered in `lib/maker/read-alignments.lua`. JSON has no comments, so notes about the IDs live here instead. Please keep extra keys out of the JSON files, because the build reads every entry as a standard.

== Scope

Our materials start at 5th grade. The Oklahoma and CSTA dictionaries do not carry standards for Pre-K through grade 4.

== Texas (`standards-tx-dictionary.json`)

IDs have the form `<course>.<knowledge statement><item>`. For example, `6.12A` is item A of statement 12 in the grade 6 standards.

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Prefix | Course

| `5`, `6`, `7`, `8` | Grade 5 through grade 8 mathematics
| `6AM`, `7AM`, `8AM` | Advanced middle school mathematics (the advanced versions of grades 6 to 8)
| `ALG1`, `ALG2` | Algebra I and Algebra II
| `GEO` | Geometry
| `Stats` | Statistics
| `MMA` | Mathematical Models with Applications
| `AQR` | Advanced Quantitative Reasoning
|===

The same standard text often appears under more than one ID, for example a grade-level standard and its advanced middle school or Algebra I counterpart. When you align lessons to one of them, check its twins. A search for identical `description` text finds them.

== Other dictionaries

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| File | ID format

| `standards-csta-dictionary.json` | `<band>-<strand>-<substrand>-<number>`, where the band is `E5` (grade 5), `MS` (middle school) or `HS` (high school), such as `MS-DAT-DI-25`
| `standards-k12cs-dictionary.json` | `<grade band>.<concept>.<subconcept>`, such as `9-12.Data and Analysis.Inference and Models`
| `standards-ia-dictionary.json` | `21.<grade band>.<strand>.<number>`, where `ES` is Employability Skills and `TL` is Technology Literacy
| `standards-ngss-dictionary.json` | `<MS or HS>-SEP<practice>-<number>` (science and engineering practices)
| `standards-ok-dictionary.json` | `OK.<grade>.<strand>...` (2022 standards)
| `standards-ok-2016-dictionary.json` | `OK.<strand>...` (2016 standards)
| `standards-ma-dictionary.json` | `<grade band>.<strand>.<letter>.<number>`, such as `9-12.CT.e.1`
| `standards-mn-dictionary.json` | `<grade>.<strand>.<standard>.<benchmark>`, such as `9.3.7.1`
| `standards-or-dictionary.json` | Common Core style IDs plus Oregon data science standards such as `HS.DR.A.1`
| `standards-cc-math-dictionary.json`, `standards-cc-ela-dictionary.json` | Common Core IDs, such as `5.OA.A.1` and `WHST.6-8.1.A`
| `ds-progressions-dictionary.json` | `<grade band>.<strand>.<substrand>.<number><letter>`, such as `9-10.C.4.5a`, plus the strand and substrand rollup rows
|===
