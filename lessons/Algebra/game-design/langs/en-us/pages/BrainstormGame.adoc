== Target

Your Player GAINS points when they hit The Target.
@vspace{3ex}

The Target is a @fitb{}{}

== Danger

Your Player LOSES points when they hit The Danger.
@vspace{3ex}

The Danger is a @fitb{}{}

The Danger moves only to the left or right.
