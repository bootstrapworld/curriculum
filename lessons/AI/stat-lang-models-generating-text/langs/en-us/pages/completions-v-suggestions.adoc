== `suggestions`

@n _Evaluate @show{(code '(suggestions m "swallowed to catch"))}._ What does the `suggestions` function do? @fitb{}{} +
@fitb{}{@ifsoln{Returns the completions for the exact phrase and each of the smaller n-grams it ends with, followed by all of the other words that are in the corpus.}}

@n What does the number in the size column tell us? @fitb{}{@ifsoln{The size of the n-gram that the suggestion or completion follows.}}

@n Why might programmers have computers generate text with `suggestions` rather than `completions`? @fitb{}{@ifsoln{It's not very helpful to not get any text at all.}}

@n Why might `suggestions` be more likely to generate nonsense than `completions`? @fitb{}{@ifsoln{It includes combinations of words that never appear in the corpus!}} +
