== Text Generation Exercise #1

@n Let's start our text generation with the word "she".

@indented{*Text Generation 1*:
@fitbruby{10em}{she}{first word} @hspace{1em}
@fitbruby{10em}{@ifsoln{swallowed}}{second word} @hspace{1em}
@fitbruby{10em}{@ifsoln{the}}{third word} @hspace{1em}
@fitbruby{10em}{@ifsoln{spider}}{fourth word}
}

@n Determine which word is most likely to complete the prompt "she" and record it on the line for second word above.

@n Now our prompt has *two words*. Find the word most likely to complete the 2-word prompt, and record it on the line above for third word.

@n How many words does our prompt have now? @fitb{2em}{@ifsoln{3}}. Use `completions` to fill in the fourth word above.

@n Everyone in your class should have generated the *same* text. Why do you think that happened?

@blanklines{3}{
@ifsoln{The corpus included a lot of repetition. Every single time we saw the word "She", it was followed by "swallowed". There were no instances when two different words had the same likelihood of following a given word.}
}

@ifsoln{The probabilities of all of the completions for each word at up to 1. For bird, the probabilities of "she" and "two" are both 0.4 with the probability of "how" trailing behind at 0.2. For cat, the probabilities of all 3 completions are equal.}
