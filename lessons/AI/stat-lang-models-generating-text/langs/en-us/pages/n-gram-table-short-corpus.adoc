== Next Word Probability

@n How many times does the word "the" appear in this corpus? @fitb{}{@ifsoln{4}}

@n How many times was the word "the" followed by the word "bird"? @fitb{}{@ifsoln{2}}

@n If you picked any of the "the"s in the corpus, what is the probability that the next word would be "bird"? @fitb{}{@ifsoln{50%}}

@n Why might it be useful to know that probability? @fitb{}{@ifsoln{we can compare it to the probability of other words following "the"}}
