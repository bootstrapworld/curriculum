*The Associative Property is true for any operation where _all groupings_ of the numbers produce the same result.*

== Is Multiplication Associative?

@n Kris thinks that multiplication is associative and draws the Circles of Evaluation below as proof. Translate the Circles of Evaluation into expressions and compute their values. 

[cols="^.^3,^.^3"]
|===
| @show{(coe '(* (* 2 5) 10))}
| @show{(coe  '(* 2 (* 5 10)))}
| @fitb{28em}{@ifsoln{@math{(2 \times 5) \times 10   =   10 \times 10   =   100}}}
| @fitb{28em}{@ifsoln{@math{2 \times (5 \times 10)   =   2 \times 50   =   100}}}
|===

@vspace{1em}

== Is Division Associative?

@n Jesse thinks that division is associative and draws the Circles of Evaluation below as proof. Translate them into expressions and evaluate them.
